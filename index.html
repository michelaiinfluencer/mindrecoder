The Neural Protocol

MindRecoder sessions use carefully designed sound environments that support relaxation, focus, and mental clarity. Certain sound patterns can influence how the brain processes rhythm and attention. Two of the most popular approaches are binaural beats and Solfeggio frequencies.

1. Binaural Beats

Binaural beats are one of the most studied sound techniques used in meditation, relaxation, and focus audio. They work by playing two slightly different tones in each ear through headphones. Your brain perceives the difference between these tones as a rhythmic pulse.

How It Works (Example):

Left ear: 200 Hz

Right ear: 210 Hz

Your brain perceives the difference as a 10 Hz beat.

Over time, the brain may begin to synchronize its activity with this rhythm, a phenomenon known as brainwave entrainment.

Brainwave Frequency Ranges

Different frequency ranges are associated with different mental states.

Beat Frequency Brain State Common Use
0.5–4 Hz Delta Deep sleep and recovery
4–8 Hz Theta Meditation and creativity
8–12 Hz Alpha Relaxation and calm focus
12–30 Hz Beta Active thinking and concentration

Research suggests that binaural beats may support relaxation, focus, sleep quality, and stress reduction, although individual results can vary. For many listeners, binaural beats create a subtle rhythmic environment that helps the mind settle into a desired mental state. MindRecoder sessions often incorporate binaural beat patterns as part of the audio background.

2. Solfeggio Frequencies

Solfeggio frequencies are specific musical tones that have been used in various spiritual and meditation traditions. These frequencies are commonly used in modern relaxation music and sound therapy environments.

While many listeners report positive experiences when listening to these tones, scientific research on their specific effects is still limited.
